# Product Guidelines

## Core Principles
1. **Clarity**: Ensure content is easy to read and understand.
2. **Speed**: The site must remain statically generated and highly performant.
3. **Dual-Environment Support**: Ensure the site functions flawlessly both on a root domain (GitHub Pages) and within a subfolder (`/on/`).

## UX & UI
- Maintain the minimalist, knowledge-graph-focused aesthetic of Obsidian.
- Preserve responsive design for all devices.
